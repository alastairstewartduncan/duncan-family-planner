# Duncan Family Planner — how to use it

The planner replaces the morning "Family daily reminders" message. Everyone sees
the same plan, gets it as a notification each morning, and timed events land in
the shared **Duncan Family** Google Calendar automatically.

## Getting started

1. Install the app (Alastair will send you the link).
2. Open it and tap **Sign in with Google**, using your own Google account.
3. Allow notifications when asked — that's how the morning plan arrives.
4. Add the widget (see below) so today's plan is always on your home screen.

If it says your email isn't on the family list, ask Alastair to add it under
**More → Family**.

## The four tabs

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Today** | The day's plan. Use the arrows to move a day at a time; the calendar icon jumps back to today. |
| **Week** | All seven days at a glance, including what's for tea. Tap a day to open it. |
| **Shopping** | A shared list everyone can add to and tick off. |
| **More** | Regular events, your usual week, reminder time, calendar and family settings. |

## Planning a day

Tap **Edit day** on any day.

- **Who's where** — for each person fill in:
  - *Where / what*: WFH, Blackburn, school, nails @ 8.30 / Preston…
  - *Extras*: anything worth highlighting, like *2nd lunch DT clinic*. Extras show
    in bold, the same way Claire bolds them in the WhatsApp message.
  - *Getting there / home*: bus home, lift home…
  Tap a suggestion chip to fill a field in one go.
- **Tea** — what's for tea, e.g. *chicken wings & waffle fries*.
- **Happening** — tap **Add something** for anything else: *Hannah*, *Ice hockey*,
  *Beavers*. Give it a time if it has one, choose who's taking or responsible,
  and who it's for.
- **Notes** — anything else everyone should know.

Tap **Save**. Everyone's app, widget and (for timed items) the family calendar
update straight away.

> **Tip:** most days start already filled in from your *usual week* and *regular
> events*, so you only change what's different.

## Who's doing what

- An item with a person's name shows who is taking it ("Alastair taking").
- If nobody is down for something, anyone can tap **I'll do it** to claim it.
  Tap **Undo** if plans change.
- Tick the box when something's done.

## Things that happen every week

**More → Regular events** holds things like ice hockey on Tuesdays at 7pm or
Beavers on Wednesdays.

- Tap **Add**, give it a name, pick the days, an optional time, who usually takes
  it and who it's for.
- It appears on every matching day for the next two weeks, and keeps rolling
  forward on its own.
- To skip it on one day, open that day, **Edit day**, and remove it with the bin
  icon. It won't come back on that day.
- Turn **Active** off to pause it (holidays), or delete it to stop it for good.

## Your usual week

**More → Usual week** sets what normally happens on each weekday — for example
Alfie: *school / bus home* Monday to Friday. Pick a day along the top, fill in each
person and tap **Save**. New days start from this. Days nobody has edited yet are
updated too.

## The morning notification

Every morning (07:00 by default) everyone gets the day's plan as a notification,
laid out just like the old WhatsApp message:

```
Wednesday 7 October
Claire – nails @ 8.30 / Preston
Alastair – WFH
Alfie – school / 2nd lunch DT clinic / bus home

Beavers
```

Pull the notification down to see it all; tap it to open that day.

- Change the time or days under **More → Morning reminder**.
- With **Tell everyone about changes** switched on, the rest of the family get a
  notification when someone edits today's or tomorrow's plan.

## The home screen widget

1. Long-press an empty spot on your home screen → **Widgets**.
2. Find **Duncan Family Planner** → drag **Today's family plan** onto the screen.
3. Resize it as you like. It shows who's where, tea and what's happening, in each
   person's colour. Tap it to open the app.

The widget refreshes when a plan changes, when the morning notification arrives,
and every half an hour.

## Google Calendar

Anything with a **time** is added to the shared *Duncan Family* calendar, with who's
taking it in the title (e.g. *Ice hockey (Alastair)*). Change or remove it in the
app and the calendar follows. Items without a time stay in the app only.

Edit events in the app, not in Google Calendar — calendar edits are overwritten
the next time the item is saved.

## Shopping list

Type an item and press enter or **+**. Tick things off as they go in the trolley,
then tap **Clear ticked**. The coloured dot shows who added it.

## Handy extras

- **⋮ → Copy as text** puts the day on the clipboard in the familiar message format,
  to paste anywhere.
- **⋮ → Copy people from previous day** fills in everyone's where/what from the day
  before — handy for a run of similar days.

## Family settings (More)

- **Family** — add someone, change a name or colour, or set the Google account they
  sign in with.
- **Google Calendar** — the shared calendar's ID (set up once by Alastair).
- **Sign out** — at the bottom.

## Questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| I didn't get this morning's notification. | Check the app's notifications are on in phone Settings, and that today is ticked under More → Morning reminder. |
| Someone's edit overwrote mine. | Two people saved the same day at once; the last save wins. Re-add your change. |
| A regular event came back after I deleted it. | Remove it from that day with **Edit day** (not from Regular events). |
| Does it work offline? | Yes for viewing; edits sync when you're back online. |
